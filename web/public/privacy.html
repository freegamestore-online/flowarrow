Privacy

Flowarrow collects no personal data.

  • The game has no analytics, tracking, advertising, or cookies.
  • Your progress and star ratings are stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes them.
  • Signing in is optional. If you sign in and post a daily result, the result is sent to the FreeGameStore leaderboard service. Nothing else leaves your device.
  • The game asks for no device permissions. It may use short vibrations for feedback where your device supports them.

Questions: see freegamestore.online.
